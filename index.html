Designed for accurate hockey analysis

Stat types stay separate so the numbers mean what they should.

Skaters and goalies are not one leaderboard

Skater scoring, possession, shooting and ice-time metrics are shown separately from goalie wins, goals-against average, save percentage and shutouts.

Transparent historical coverage

Traditional stats and advanced reports do not have identical historical coverage. Missing fields remain unavailable rather than being silently treated as zero.
