Prediction methodology

TRANSPARENT MODEL
Regular-season projection
Current points per game multiplied by 82. Teams with few games played have more volatile projections.
Playoff qualification
Division ranking plus wild-card ranking using projected points, then goal differential as a tie-breaker.
Series picks
The higher projected seed advances. This is a simple baseline, not a probability-calibrated playoff simulator.
Draft order
The bottom 16 projected teams are shown in reverse points pace. Lottery odds, trades and conditional picks are not modelled.
