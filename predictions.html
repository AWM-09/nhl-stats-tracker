Prediction methodology

TRANSPARENT MODEL
Pre-game strength
Points per game and goal differential per game, calculated only from earlier completed games.
Game forecast
A logistic win-probability model with a modest home-ice adjustment and a score estimate based on prior scoring and goals allowed.
Projected points
Current points plus expected points from every remaining regular-season game, including a small expected overtime-loss allowance.
Playoff bracket
Three automatic qualifiers per division plus two wild cards per conference. Picks are editable and advance through the bracket.
Independent fan-made model. NHL schedule and standings data: NHL Web API snapshots. Predictions are not betting advice or official NHL forecasts.
