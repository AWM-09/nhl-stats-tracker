Data quality rules

Missing is not zero
Unavailable source fields stay blank. Do not infer a zero from missing data.
Skaters and goalies stay separate
Their role-specific statistics use different reports and must not be ranked together.
Historical coverage varies
Advanced tracking metrics may be unavailable in earlier seasons.
Check source response, not only deployment
A green Pages deployment means the static files were published; it does not prove NHL APIs returned data.
